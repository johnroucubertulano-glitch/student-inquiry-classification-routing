# Methodology-Selection Justification

We selected **Scrum, an Agile/Adaptive methodology**, for our Machine Learning-Based Student Inquiry Classification and Routing System. The project has a clear scope, including inquiry submission, classification, routing, responses, and feedback, but some requirements may change based on testing and adviser feedback. The ML classification component also has technical risk because accuracy and routing results require repeated testing and improvement. Our semester schedule has fixed milestones, while our team can divide tasks and coordinate during regular sprint activities. Scrum is more appropriate than **Waterfall** because it can accommodate changes, and more appropriate than **Kanban** because our project has planned semester deliverables and benefits from structured sprints. SDLC activities such as requirements, design, development, testing, and deployment will be completed through short sprints, with reviews and feedback guiding the next cycle. Git/GitHub and CI/CD will support version control, integration, testing, and delivery. However, Scrum requires consistent teamwork and communication, which the team must manage to avoid delayed sprint goals.
